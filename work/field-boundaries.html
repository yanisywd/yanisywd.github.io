Field boundaries from one click

A farmer clicks once inside a field, and the system returns its outline. SAM2, Meta’s general segmentation model, produces the candidate masks. Its highest-scored mask often locks onto a texture inside the field, so I added a multi-prompt stability vote that rejects it.

Status
In production at MyEasyFarm, as an interactive map tool
My role
Design, code and evaluation
Stack
Python, PyTorch, SAM 2.1, rasterio, Shapely
Data
IGN aerial photos (20 cm) and declared parcels (RPG), France

How it works

  1. Click. The system gets the coordinates, the declared parcel around them (RPG, through the IGN API) and a 20 cm aerial photo of the area (IGN Géoplateforme).
  2. Prompt. SAM 2.1 gets the click as a point prompt and returns 3 candidate masks with scores.
  3. Stability vote. I re-prompt at 6 points within 30 m of the click and keep the candidate that stays the same across prompts. Texture traps, like soil strips or growth zones, change from one prompt to the next, so they lose.
  4. Refine. A second pass refines the winner. When a declared parcel exists, a box prompt from its bounds snaps the declared shape to the visible edges.
  5. Output. A clean polygon in GeoJSON, with its area in hectares.

15 random fields of 0.5 ha or more in 5 French regions, compared with the 2024 declarations. IoU (intersection over union) measures the overlap of two shapes: 1 is a perfect match. A larger model is not the lever. The remaining errors are boundaries that exist on paper but not on the ground, and neighbouring fields of the same crop.

Limits

  • The RPG is a declaration, not visual truth. One uniform block is often several declared strips with no visible edge, so IoU against it understates the visual quality.
  • Aerial photos are refreshed every 3 to 4 years, so a photo can be older than the declaration.
  • Neighbouring fields of the same crop, separated by a thin path, can merge. Next step: fine-tune a field model (AI4Boundaries or Fields of the World) and keep SAM2 as the interactive refiner.
  • Outside France there is no RPG. The system can use FTW Global, an open map of 1.6 billion field polygons, as the guide instead.
