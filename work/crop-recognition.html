Recognising crops from space

Which crop grows in each field? I tested ESA’s WorldCereal, a model pretrained on millions of labelled fields, in 7 regions of 6 countries without retraining it. Then I added a confidence gate: it commits only above a threshold and routes the rest to human review.

Status
In production at MyEasyFarm, first deployed on 7,020 fields near Reims
My role
Input pipeline, evaluation, decision rule and demo
Stack
Python, PyTorch, WorldCereal (Presto)
Data
Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, Copernicus elevation and NASA POWER weather, all open data

How it works

  1. Open data. For each region, 12 monthly composites of Sentinel-2 (optical) and Sentinel-1 (radar) at 20 m, plus elevation and weather. The reference pipeline runs this step on a cloud platform. I re-implemented it on open public catalogues, so the whole chain runs on one laptop.
  2. Prediction. 16 random pixels per field, at least 20 m from the border. WorldCereal (a Presto encoder and a crop head) gives one of 24 crop classes per pixel, and the answers are averaged per field.
  3. Decision. Accept the answer when the confidence is 0.9 or more. Send the other fields to review.
  4. Option. Where last year’s declarations are public, a small regional classifier gives a second opinion.

What I found

  • A probable bug in WorldCereal’s class tables. The code for “barley, unspecified” points to “wheat”. This explains why 28 of 29 barley fields in North Dakota came out as wheat, while European barley was 96 to 97% right.
  • Some confident errors are label errors. Three Reims fields declared as winter peas look like sunflower from space: their greenness peaks in July and August. After a very wet spring, the farmer probably sowed a summer crop instead.
  • An input bug. A double reflectance correction held Reims at 0.17 accuracy. Once fixed, it reached 0.93.
  • Local labels help where the model is weak. A classifier trained on last year’s declarations took Flevoland from 0.78 to 0.88, and to 0.895 with the confidence rule on top.

Limits

  • Permanent and temporary grassland look the same from space.
  • Fields under about 1 ha are not reliable.
  • In a region unlike the training data, the 0.9 rule is less safe (90% in Tuscany), so each new region needs a check before use.
